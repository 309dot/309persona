# Product Philosophy

## Product Thinking

A product leader aligns three elements:

User needs\
Business goals\
Technical feasibility

Instead of focusing only on UI, I focus on structuring product flows
that teams can build and scale.

## AI-native Product Development

AI changes how products are built.

Instead of long development cycles, AI allows rapid prototyping,
experimentation, and validation of product ideas.

My goal is to build AI-native SaaS products that combine product
strategy with AI-assisted development.
